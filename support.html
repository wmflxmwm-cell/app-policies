Game Night Atlas

Privacy Policy

Can I back up or restore a game?

No. Games, scores and results are never saved, so each game ends when you close it. The only things the app keeps are your sound setting and, if you turn on Remember player names, the player names. These are included in your normal iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) and come back if you restore your iPhone from that backup. There is no separate backup file.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. The app never connects to the internet and sends nothing to us or anyone else.

How do I remove remembered player names?

In the app's Settings, turn off Remember player names. This deletes the saved names right away. Or tap Forget saved names to clear them and keep the setting on.

My game disappeared when I closed the app.

That is expected. Games are kept only while they are on screen and are not restored after the app is closed.

I can't hear the sound effects.

Check that Sound effects is on in the app's Settings. Sound effects follow your iPhone's ringer and Silent setting.

Deckling

Privacy Policy

How do I back up my cards?

On the app's Backup screen, tap Create Backup File, then choose where to keep it, for example Save to Files and then iCloud Drive. The file contains all your decks, notes, cards, review history and photos. It is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere private. Make a new backup now and then: deleting the app deletes your cards.

How do I restore a backup?

On the Backup screen, choose to restore and pick your Deckling backup file (.json). Restoring replaces all decks and cards currently on this iPhone with the ones in the file.

Where is my data stored? Does it sync with iCloud?

Your data is stored only on your iPhone. It does not sync with iCloud, and nothing is sent to us. Your normal iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone includes it. To move to a new iPhone, restore your iPhone backup on the new phone, or create a Deckling backup file and restore it there.

Can I import cards from a spreadsheet?

Yes. Save your cards as a CSV or TSV file (up to 20 MB) and use the app's import option to pick the file.

The daily reminder doesn't appear.

Make sure Daily Reminder is on in the app's settings and that notifications are allowed for Deckling in iOS Settings > Notifications.

Take Photo doesn't work.

Camera access may be off. Turn it on for Deckling in iOS Settings, or use Choose Photo to pick an existing photo instead.

Cursive Kitchen

Privacy Policy · Terms of Use

How do I back up my recipes?

Tap Create Backup File, then save the file somewhere safe, such as Files or iCloud Drive. The file contains your recipes (including originals and cook history), collections, grocery list and photos. Running timers are not included. It is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere private. Recipes are stored only on this iPhone, so deleting the app deletes them. Keep a backup file.

How do I restore a backup?

Tap Restore from Backup File, pick your Cursive Kitchen backup file (.json) and confirm. Restoring replaces all recipes currently on this iPhone with the ones in the file.

Where is my data stored? Does it sync with iCloud?

Your recipes are stored only on your iPhone. They do not sync with iCloud, and nothing is sent to us. Your normal iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone includes them. To move to a new iPhone, restore your iPhone backup on the new phone, or use a Cursive Kitchen backup file.

How do I manage or cancel Cursive Kitchen Pro?

In the app's Settings, tap Manage Subscription. Or, on your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, then choose Cursive Kitchen Pro. To avoid being charged for the next month, cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date. Pro stays active until the end of the period you have paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?

Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe, then use the restore option in the app's Settings or on the Pro screen. If Pro still does not show, connect to the internet so the app can check with the App Store, and try again.

How do I save a recipe from Safari or another app?

Tap the Share button, then Save to Cursive Kitchen. The link or text is saved to the app's Inbox. Nothing is downloaded until you open it in Cursive Kitchen and tap Import Recipe. The app then downloads that one page directly from the website.

Why can't I add or remove grocery list items?

The grocery list is part of Cursive Kitchen Pro. Adding items (from a recipe or by typing them), removing items, Remove Checked Items and Clear List need an active subscription. Without Pro, an existing list can still be viewed, checked off and shared.

My timer didn't alert me.

Timer alerts need notifications to be allowed for Cursive Kitchen in iOS Settings > Notifications. If they are off, timers ring only while the app is open.

Purchases and refunds

Game Night Atlas and Deckling are paid apps, and Cursive Kitchen offers the Cursive Kitchen Pro subscription. All payments go through Apple's App Store. We cannot see your payment details and cannot issue refunds ourselves.

To request a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account, and choose the purchase. Apple reviews refund requests under its own policies.
