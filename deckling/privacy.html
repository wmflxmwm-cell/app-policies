Deckling

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

This policy explains how the Deckling flashcard app for iPhone handles information. Deckling is made by an independent developer (“we”, “us”).

The short version

  • We do not collect any personal data. Your decks, cards, photos and study history stay on your iPhone and are never sent to us.
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. The app itself never connects to the internet, and it does not sync to iCloud.
  • Photo to Cards (text recognition) and read-aloud run on your iPhone using Apple's built-in technology.
  • Your data leaves your iPhone only if you choose to share a backup file, or as part of your own iPhone backups.
  • The camera and notifications are used only when you choose a feature that needs them.

1. What is stored on your iPhone

Deckling stores your study data in its own private storage on your iPhone. Other apps cannot read it, and it is not synced to iCloud or any server.

  • Decks: the name, study settings (such as daily limits for new and review cards, learning steps, target recall and maximum interval), the read-aloud languages you chose, whether the deck is archived, and when it was created.
  • Notes and cards: the text you write for the front and back, the card type, the read-aloud language, and when the note was created and last changed.
  • Scheduling: for each card, when it is next due, the values the scheduler uses (such as stability, difficulty, number of reviews and lapses), and whether it is suspended or buried.
  • Review history: each rating you give, when you gave it, the study day (a study day starts at 4 a.m. local time), how long you spent on the card (counted up to 10 minutes), whether the card was new, and the card's schedule before and after. This is used for scheduling and for your study statistics, on your iPhone.
  • Photos you attach to notes (one per note, taken with the camera or chosen with the photo picker): the app saves a new, resized copy (up to 2048 pixels on the longest side, as a JPEG). It does not copy the original file, and it never saves anything to your Photos library.
  • Daily reminder: whether it is on, and the time you chose.
  • Housekeeping for safe restores: a random ID for your data set, and during a restore, working copies of your data and a small status file. If a restored file fails the app's checks, the app keeps that copy aside in its private storage. It is not deleted automatically and is removed when you delete the app.

Things kept only while you use them

  • Photo to Cards: the photo you take or choose is held in memory only while that screen is open. Only the lines of text you pick become notes. The photo itself is not saved or attached.
  • Importing from a CSV or TSV file: the preview is held in memory. Only the cards you import are saved.
  • Undo Last Rating: the undo step is kept in memory and cleared when the app closes.

Backup files

When you tap Create Backup File, the app creates a file with all your decks, notes, cards, review history and photos, plus the app's identifier and version and the time of export. The file is not encrypted. It is created in the app's temporary storage and replaced the next time you create a backup, and iOS may also clear it. It leaves the app only if you save or send it somewhere using the share sheet.

Reminder notification

If the Daily Reminder is on, iOS stores one repeating notification on your iPhone with fixed text (“Time to study” and “A few minutes of review keeps your cards fresh.”) and the time you chose. It contains none of your cards.

2. What leaves your iPhone

The app sends nothing to us. Deckling contains no code that connects to the internet. There is no account, no server and no iCloud sync.

Backup files you share

After you create a backup, the share sheet lets you save it to Files or iCloud Drive, or send it with AirDrop, Mail, Messages or another app. The file goes only to the place you choose. We do not receive it. Because it contains all your cards, review history and photos and is not encrypted, keep it somewhere private.

Files you open

To restore a backup or import cards from a CSV or TSV file, you pick a file with the system file picker. The app can read only the file you pick. If that file is in iCloud Drive or another storage service, iOS may download it from that service.

Text recognition, read-aloud and language detection

Photo to Cards reads text from your photo using Apple's Vision framework on your iPhone. Read-aloud uses the voices installed on your iPhone, and the app detects the language of your text on your iPhone. The app does not send your photos or text anywhere.

Daily reminder

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. The app does not receive push notifications from any server.

Privacy Policy link

Tapping Privacy Policy in the app's Settings > About opens this page in your web browser. This page is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub receives the information any website receives, such as your IP address and browser type. The app adds no information about you to the link. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Support email

The app shows our support address as text. The app does not send email. If you write to us from your own email app, we receive your email address and whatever you include, and we use it only to answer you. We keep these emails only as long as we need them to help you, and we delete them within 12 months after your question is resolved, or sooner if you ask. Our email is provided by Google (Gmail).

Buying the app

Deckling is a paid download. Apple handles the purchase through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. Apple gives developers sales reports and statistics that do not identify you. The app has no in-app purchases and does not check your purchase. Apple's handling of your information is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

iPhone backups

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, iOS includes Deckling's data (your decks, cards, review history, photos and settings) in that backup, as it does for most apps. The temporary backup file is not included. You and Apple control these backups. We have no access to them.

Crash reports shared by Apple

If you have chosen in iOS to share analytics with app developers (Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give us crash reports and usage statistics that do not identify you. You can turn this off at any time. The app itself contains no crash-reporting or analytics code.

3. Permissions

  • Camera, only when you tap Take Photo: in Photo to Cards, to photograph your notes or a textbook and turn the text into flashcards (that photo is processed on your iPhone and is not kept), or in the note editor, to attach a photo to a note (the app keeps a resized copy in its own storage, as described in “What is stored on your iPhone”). Photos are never saved to your Photos library. If you decline, the app does not ask again. You can change this later in iOS Settings.
  • Notifications, only when you turn on Daily Reminder. They are used only for the daily study reminder. (The request includes badges, but the app never sets one.)
  • Photos: the app has no access to your photo library. Choose Photo uses Apple's photo picker, which gives the app only the photo you select.
  • Files: the system file picker gives the app only the file you select.
  • Not used: microphone, location, contacts, speech recognition, Bluetooth, local network and tracking.

4. Ads, analytics and third-party code

  • No ads, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools.
  • The app does not use the advertising identifier or any other identifier to recognize you or your device.
  • No third-party SDKs. The scheduler is our own Swift version of the open-source FSRS algorithm (based on py-fsrs, MIT License). It is code inside the app and sends no data.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

5. Deleting your information

Everything is on your iPhone, so you delete it there:

  • Delete a deck: swipe it or press and hold it, then confirm twice. This removes the deck and all its notes, cards, review history and photos.
  • Delete a note: swipe it on the deck screen, or tap Delete Note in the editor, then confirm. This removes its cards, their review history and its photo.
  • Edit a note: removing its photo deletes the photo file. Removing a reverse card or a cloze deletes those cards and their review history after you confirm.
  • Undo Last Rating removes that review from your history.
  • Turn off Daily Reminder to remove the scheduled notification. The reminder time setting stays until you delete the app.
  • Restoring a backup replaces all your current data. After the restored data passes the app's checks, the old data is deleted.
  • Archiving a deck does not delete it.
  • Everything: there is no single “erase all” button. Delete the app to remove everything it stored on your iPhone, including photos, settings, any backup file in temporary storage and any copy kept from a failed restore.

Backup files you saved or sent elsewhere, and your iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone, are outside the app. Delete them where you keep them. We keep no copy of your information, so there is nothing to ask us to delete.

6. Children

Deckling is not directed at children under 13 and is not part of the App Store's Kids category. The app does not collect personal information from anyone, of any age, so we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Anything a person types into cards or attaches as a photo stays on the iPhone unless they choose to share a backup file.

If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your choices and rights

Because the app sends us nothing, we hold no personal data from it, so there is nothing for us to give you access to, correct, export or delete. You can export all of your own data at any time with Create Backup File. If you have emailed us, you can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

8. Changes
