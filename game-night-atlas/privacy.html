Game Night Atlas

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

This policy explains how the Game Night Atlas app for iPhone handles information. Game Night Atlas is made by an independent developer (“we”, “us”).

The short version

  • We do not collect any personal data. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code. The app itself never connects to the internet.
  • Games, scores and results are never saved. Player names are saved only if you turn on Remember player names, and only on your iPhone.
  • The app asks for no permissions: no camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location or notifications.

1. What is stored on your iPhone

Game Night Atlas keeps only these items, in the app's own private settings on your iPhone. Other apps cannot read them.

  • Sound effects: on or off (on unless you change it).
  • Remember player names: on or off (off unless you turn it on).
  • Saved player names, only if Remember player names is on. When you tap Start game, the names you entered are saved so they can fill in the next setup screen. Up to 16 names are kept, each up to 20 characters. Names used in the sample round are never saved.

If Remember player names is off, no names are kept.

The game content is built into the app. The app only reads it and never changes it.

2. What is never saved

Everything about a game in progress exists only in the iPhone's memory while the game is on screen: player names, teams, roles, secrets, scores, results, and game options such as Hard mode, time limits and the number of rounds. It is thrown away when you finish or end the game. If the app is closed, the game is not restored.

3. What leaves your iPhone

The app sends nothing. Game Night Atlas contains no code that connects to the internet, so it cannot send your names, games or settings anywhere.

A few things happen outside the app, either only when you choose them or as part of how Apple's services work:

Privacy Policy link

Tapping Privacy Policy in the app's Settings > About opens this page in your web browser. This page is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub receives the information any website receives, such as your IP address and browser type. The app adds no information about you to the link. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Support email

The app shows our support address as text. The app does not send email. If you write to us from your own email app, we receive your email address and whatever you include, and we use it only to answer you. We keep these emails only as long as we need them to help you, and we delete them within 12 months after your question is resolved, or sooner if you ask. Our email is provided by Google (Gmail).

Buying the app

Game Night Atlas is a paid download. Apple handles the purchase through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. Apple gives developers sales reports and statistics that do not identify you. The app has no in-app purchases and does not check your purchase. Apple's handling of your information is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

iPhone backups

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, iOS includes the app's settings and any saved player names in that backup, as it does for most apps. You and Apple control these backups. We have no access to them.

Crash reports shared by Apple

If you have chosen in iOS to share analytics with app developers (Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give us crash reports and usage statistics that do not identify you. You can turn this off at any time. The app itself contains no crash-reporting or analytics code.

Things that stay on your iPhone

Sound effects, vibrations and VoiceOver announcements are handled by iOS on your iPhone. Nothing is sent.

4. Permissions

Game Night Atlas asks for no permissions. It does not use the camera, microphone, photo library, contacts, location, Bluetooth, local network or notifications, and it never asks to track you.

Not a permission, but good to know: while a game is running and not paused, the app keeps the screen from turning off. Sound effects follow your ringer and Silent setting.

5. Ads, analytics and tracking

  • No ads.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools, and no third-party code of any kind. The app uses only Apple's own system frameworks.
  • The app does not use the advertising identifier or any other identifier to recognize you or your device. Players in a game get random IDs that exist only in memory and are never saved or sent.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

6. Deleting your information

  • Saved player names: in the app's Settings, turn off Remember player names. This deletes the saved names right away. You can also tap Forget saved names and leave the setting on.
  • Games, scores and results: there is nothing to delete, because they are never saved.
  • Everything: delete the app. iOS removes everything the app stored on your iPhone. Copies in earlier iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone remain until those backups are replaced or deleted.
  • We keep no copy of your information, so there is nothing to ask us to delete.

7. Children

Game Night Atlas is not directed at children under 13 and is not part of the App Store's Kids category. The app does not collect personal information from anyone, of any age, so we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Player names typed into the app may include children's names. They stay on the iPhone and are saved only if Remember player names is on.

If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your choices and rights

Because the app sends us nothing, we hold no personal data from it, so there is nothing for us to give you access to, correct, export or delete. Your information stays under your control on your iPhone, as described above. If you have emailed us, you can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date at the top. If a future version of the app handles information differently, we will update this policy before that version is released.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy: wmflxmwm@gmail.com. You can also visit the support page.
