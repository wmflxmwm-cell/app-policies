2. Free features

Cursive Kitchen is free to download and use, and it does not need an account. Some features require an active Cursive Kitchen Pro subscription. Without Pro:

  • you can keep up to 25 recipes;
  • you can save 3 scanned recipes and 3 translated recipes on each device (a scan or translation counts only when you save the recipe);
  • you can run one cooking timer at a time; and
  • you can't add items to the grocery list or remove them. An existing list can still be viewed, checked off and shared.

If your subscription ends, Pro features stop working, but the app does not delete your recipes. All of them stay available, but if you have 25 or more you can't add new ones until you subscribe again.

3. Cursive Kitchen Pro subscription

Your recipes are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app, or losing or resetting your iPhone, can mean losing them. Use Create Backup File regularly and keep the file somewhere safe. We never have a copy of your data, so we cannot recover it for you.

6. Accuracy and food safety

Recipe scanning, web import, translation, unit display and timer suggestions are automatic and can make mistakes. Always check ingredients, quantities, temperatures, cooking times and allergens against the original recipe, and use your own judgment about food safety.

Timer alerts depend on notifications being allowed in iOS. If they are not allowed, timers ring only while the app is open. Do not rely on the app as your only reminder for anything where safety matters.
